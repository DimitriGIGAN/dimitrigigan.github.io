    What a health mutual migration involves

    My role

    • Data takeover, checks and cleanup, before and after the switchover
    • Configuration of more than 500 benefits in ASSIA
    • Took part in switching the data flows over
    • Set up the extranets
    • Set up the accounting exchange with slip generation
    • More than 100 queries and extractions to check and monitor activity
    • User support and coaching during and after the change

    Sample check

    A simplified, anonymised example of the kind of query used to make sure no member got lost during the takeover.

    -- Members present in the old system but missing from the new one
SELECT a.num_adherent, a.nom, a.date_adhesion
FROM   anciens_adherents a
LEFT JOIN adherents_assia n
       ON n.num_adherent = a.num_adherent
WHERE  n.num_adherent IS NULL;

    The result: the mutual changed software in 6 months without losing its data, and the teams had mastered the tool a year later.
